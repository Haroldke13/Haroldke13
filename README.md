### About

Statistician and IT practitioner, working in data and IT since 2016. My
background is public-sector statistical research, health records, digitisation
and technical support — and I build the software to go with it: Flask web
applications, data pipelines, computer vision, local speech processing and
structured research workflows.

I've spent most of my career inside regulated institutions — a national health
insurer, a teaching and referral hospital, the national statistics bureau, the
Judiciary, and now a sector regulator. That shapes how I build: identity checks
at every handover, auditable access, and accuracy treated as the requirement
rather than a nice-to-have.

I like problems that run end to end. Not just the model or the endpoint, but the
data cleaning before it, the interface on top of it, and the thing a
non-technical colleague actually clicks on Monday morning. I use AI as a tool
rather than a slogan: LLMs in the development loop, and shipped features built
on transcription, translation and vision models.
